# Brand selection

## Selected

`01-minimal-receipt-check.png`

The receipt and check mark is the clearest link to BillMinder for Android. It stays legible at small sizes, avoids generic monitor imagery, and looks appropriate beside a Windows app name. The mark uses true transparency and keeps a simple color system that already appears in the product.

## Supporting option

`03-wordmark.png` is retained as a concept. It is useful for wide placements, but the heavier outline makes it less flexible than the selected symbol.

## Rejected as primary marks

- `02-app-window-receipt.png` reads too much like a browser icon.
- `04-monitor-emblem.png` is clear but generic.
- `05-abstract-desktop-receipt.png` loses the receipt silhouette at small sizes.

All five originals are preserved in `generated/`.
